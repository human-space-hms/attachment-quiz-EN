Scientific Basis

This quiz is conceptually informed by adult attachment research, including John Bowlby's attachment theory, Mary Ainsworth's early attachment studies, Cindy Hazan and Phillip Shaver's work extending attachment theory to adult relationships, Kim Bartholomew and Leonard Horowitz's four-category model, and the Anxiety × Avoidance framework developed by Brennan, Clark, and Shaver for the ECR / ECR-R.

This is not the official ECR. It has not been clinically or psychometrically validated.
